# Code of Conduct

This project follows the principles of the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>).

## Our standard

Be respectful and professional. Criticize ideas and code, not people. Assume good faith, give
and accept feedback gracefully, and keep discussion focused on the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling,
publishing others' private information, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that violate
this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they judge harmful.

Report violations to the maintainer, Hyrum Hendrickson, through the contact details on his GitHub
profile (<https://github.com/HyrumHendrickson>). Reports are handled confidentially.
